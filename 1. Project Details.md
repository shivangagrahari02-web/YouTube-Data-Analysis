Project Description

This project is an end-to-end exploratory data analysis case study focused on YouTube India trending video data. The objective was to identify patterns influencing video virality, audience engagement, and content publishing strategies. Using Python libraries such as Pandas and Matplotlib in Jupyter Notebook, the dataset was cleaned, transformed, analyzed, and visualized to derive actionable insights related to YouTube trending behavior.

Goal of the Project

The goal of this project was to analyze YouTube India trending video data to understand the factors responsible for videos trending on YouTube. The analysis focused on identifying the impact of real-time content, upload timing, and seasonal publishing trends on channel performance and audience engagement.

Problem Statements

Problem Statement 1: Same-Day Trending Analysis

Identify patterns behind videos that appear on the Trending page on the same day they are uploaded. The objective was to understand whether specific content categories or upload strategies increase the probability of immediate trending.

Problem Statement 2: T-Series Channel Analysis

Analyze the upload strategy of the T-Series YouTube channel to understand how seasonal publishing behavior impacts engagement and visibility
